# Strategy audit

Generated by `node tools/build-strategy.js` in 2442 seconds, with Stockfish 19 (the build MyChess uses).

## How the exercises were chosen

1. From the end of each of the 3865 distinct Lichess opening positions, Stockfish played on for up to 40 plies (depth 12), giving 152303 positions. Only positions from move 10 on are used, and positions from the 29 main openings of the Theory course are tried first.
2. A position became a candidate when Stockfish’s move matched a lesson’s rule in `js/strategy-rules.js`. The rules are exact, for example: *an outpost is a square in the opponent’s half, protected by your pawn, that no enemy pawn can ever attack*. Moves that give check or promote are left out: those are tactics.
3. Each candidate was searched again with Stockfish’s three best moves (depth 18). It was kept only if the best move is still the lesson’s idea, every move that is not the idea is at least 2.5 points of winning chance worse, and the position is not already decided (within ±3 pawns; ±9 for “trade when ahead”). At most one exercise comes from each opening line, and four from each opening family.
4. Every exercise kept was searched once more at depth 22, and dropped if Stockfish changed its mind or the deeper search found the position already decided.

In the app, the answer is Stockfish’s move. Any other move that shows the same idea and is within 1.5 points of it also counts as right; anything else is checked by Stockfish live and explained.
